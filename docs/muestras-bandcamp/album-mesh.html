          about

          The mini-album "Fallen Angel" follows in the wake of MESH's current album "The Truth Doesn't Matter", which has already been reprinted several times. The band's masterminds, Mark Hockings and Richard Silverthorn, felt that they had some unfinished business with their latest record. Following the release of their critically acclaimed full-length that has scored several 'Album of the Month' accolades and a first leg of touring with many sold-out shows, the duo was extremely motivated to start writing again. New material soon emerged and the tracks felt more like an addendum to "The Truth Doesn't Matter" than the beginning of a new chapter. MESH decided to release those new tracks in combination with remixes and live recordings as a mini-album. "Fallen Angel" offers a deeper dive into the themes and concepts of "The Truth Doesn't Matter". "Fallen Angel" revisits the world of MESH's acclaimed album in a way that resembles a DLC in gaming by extending the original story and adding exciting new chapters. You wanted more MESH? You get more MESH!

          Mesh Bristol, UK

          MESH is an electronic alternative band from Bristol consisting of Mark Hockings and Richard Silverthorn. They made their baby steps as a band back in 1996 by releasing their first album on Memento Materia.
          In the meantime they have accumulated a large fanbase throughout Europe with their feature sound that draws influences from Electronic Pop music as well as from Alternative and Industrial.           ... more
